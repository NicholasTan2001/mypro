MyProfile Documentation

1. What is MyProfile?
MyProfile is a web application that allows users to manage their profiles and can be share to public.

2. Can i change any language in MyProfile?
Yes, you can change any language at the right side of navigation bar.

3. How to login in MyProfile?
You may click login button on the right side of navigation bar. Then, insert your identity number and passwword to login.

4. How to register in MyProfile?
You may click the register button at login button. The, insert some user's information to register as new user in MyProfile.

5. What is home page in MyProfile?
Home Page in MyProfile is about the introduction for MyProfile web application.

6. What is about us page in MyProfile?
About Us Page in MyProfile is about the MyProfile web application's details.

7. What is MyProfile page in MyProfile?
You may insert, update and delete your information. So, public can view it.

8. What is search page in MyProfile?
You may find another user's information by searching their name.

9. What is Friends Page in MyProfile?
You can view your friend's profile without any visibility settings. Meanwhile, you can accpect/reject their friend's requests and remove them from friends list.

10. What is Settings Page in MyProfile?
You can send bluetick badge verification request, active notification, modify visibility settings, update new password and delete your account permanently at Settings Page.

11. What is Activity History Page in Myprofile?
You may check your account's activity history record in this page. So, you will know what happen to this account previously.
